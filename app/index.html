Deal Scout

Real deals, checked against named sources, with an honest verdict on each one. No inflated was-prices, no filler.

How these prices were checked

Every deal lists its store, its sale price, its usual price, the discount between them, and the source that reported the price. The verdict on each card says whether the cut is a new low, a strong price, a decent cut or a modest one, and on what evidence. Store links go to the store's own search for the product. Deal Scout takes no payment from any store.
